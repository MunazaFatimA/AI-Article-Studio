# ArticleForge - AI Article Studio

A powerful AI-powered article generation system with multi-model support, content humanization, and AI detection.

##  Features

-**Multi-Model AI Generation** - Support for Groq, OpenRouter, Gemini, Cohere, Tavily
-**Content Humanization** - Advanced NLP-based humanization with 500+ patterns
-**AI Detection** - Multi-technique detection (Perplexity, Burstiness, TTR, etc.)
-**Model Comparison** - Compare outputs from different AI models
-**Auto Image Integration** - Fetch relevant images automatically
-**DOCX Export** - Export articles with images and formatting
-**Zero AI Score** - Humanize content to pass AI detection
